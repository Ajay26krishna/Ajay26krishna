## Research Experience

At Penn State, I developed machine-learning and research-software workflows for constrained optimization, simulation, and performance evaluation.

- **Constraint-aware chiplet placement:** Developed graph-based reinforcement-learning workflows using **PyTorch, PPO, imitation learning, and legal-action masking** for joint wirelength and thermal optimization. Evaluated zero-shot placement on unseen designs, with benchmark results of up to **27% lower wirelength** and **58× faster time-to-solution** compared with simulated annealing.
- **Oscillator-based Ising machines:** Developed **C++/MPI/OpenMP** experiments and **GNN-assisted node-freezing** methods for Max-Cut optimization. Across seven MQLib graphs, the evaluated approach reduced runtime by **33.8% on average**, with **0.122% median Max-Cut loss**.

**Publication:** Co-author, *“Constraint-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Joint Thermal and Wirelength Optimization in Chiplet Placement”* — **submitted to DAC 2027**.
